Tony Shin

Founder | Chef | Artist

Tony Shin is a photographer with a background in art and marketing. His work looks closely at everyday life — the table after a meal, the light on a farm, the quiet of a studio, and the hours that usually pass unnoticed. Day In Frame is his journal of that practice.

Each frame is chosen with care. The work is not a stream of snapshots, but a record of how ordinary life looks when someone stays long enough to see it.

Tony Uncle Kimchi

Sharing Healthy K-kimchi

He began making kimchi with his mother, a professional kimchi maker, when he was very young. In recent years he has grown the ingredients himself on his father’s farm.

He holds Korea’s national chef certification and license, and works with kimchi as well as Korean cuisine. He is now beginning his kimchi career.
