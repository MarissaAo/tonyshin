Tony Shin

Entrepreneur | Chef | Artist

Tony Shin is an exhibition design specialist and photographer who has travelled across more than 50 countries, capturing the beauty of the world through his lens and discovering inspiration in different cultures.

With a creative mind and a passion for business, he later co-founded a marketing strategy company with friends, helping businesses develop effective strategies and increase their revenue.

His love for cooking began in childhood, inspired by his mother's exceptional culinary skills. Over the years, he explored traditional Korean cuisine while travelling the world and experiencing its diverse food cultures.

As artificial intelligence began transforming the marketing industry, Tony saw an opportunity to pursue a new direction. Driven by his lifelong passion for food, he successfully obtained a professional Korean cuisine chef qualification in South Korea and embarked on a new chapter in the culinary world.

For Tony, life is a journey of discovery, creativity, and the courage to embrace new beginnings.

Each frame is chosen with care. The work is not a stream of snapshots, but a record of how ordinary life looks when someone stays long enough to see it.

Tony Uncle Kimchi

Sharing Healthy K-kimchi

Rooted in Korean tradition and inspired by a mother's homemade recipes, Tony Uncle Kimchi was born from a lifelong love of authentic Korean kimchi.

Having learned the art of kimchi-making from his mother since childhood, Tony has carried this tradition with him around the world, sharing the joy of making and enjoying kimchi with people from different cultures.

Today, Tony Uncle Kimchi brings this passion to life through a variety of thoughtfully crafted kimchi, with a simple belief: healthy, delicious kimchi should be something everyone can enjoy.

From children to seniors, our mission is to share the goodness of Korean kimchi with every generation.
